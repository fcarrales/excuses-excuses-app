## Full description

**Excuses, Excuses!** helps you write respectful, ready-to-send messages when you do not know what to say.

Whether you are running late, canceling plans, rescheduling, sending a work message, apologizing, or navigating dating and family conversations — pick a situation, choose your tone, and get three polished text options you can copy or share instantly.

**Built for real life**

- Message generator for common awkward moments
- English, Spanish, and Spanglish built in
- Saved people — personalize tone per relationship
- Message Coach — clarity and respect tips on every result
- Style presets for your favorite combinations
- Favorites and history — all stored on your device
- Install like an app (PWA) — no app store required for web beta

**Privacy-first**

No account required. No ads. No tracking in this beta. Your messages and saved data stay in your browser unless you choose to export a backup.

**Safety**

Excuses, Excuses! is a message assistant — not a document generator. It does **not** create fake proof, fake documents, fake receipts, doctor notes, screenshots, or official-looking records. Risky requests are blocked.

Perfect for polite replies, work messages, school messages, and everyday social situations.
