# Safety Policy — Excuses, Excuses!

**Last updated:** Beta v0.8.0

---

## Purpose

Excuses, Excuses! is a **message assistant** for awkward social situations, work messages, school messages, and everyday texting. It helps you write **respectful, ready-to-send messages** — not fake proof or official documents.

## What the app is for

- Polite replies when you are running late or need to cancel
- Work and school messages that are clear and professional
- Apologies, soft nos, and rescheduling texts
- Family, dating, and social situations
- English, Spanish, and Spanglish message options

## What the app refuses

The app **blocks** requests related to:

- Fake proof, fake documents, or fake receipts
- Doctor notes, medical excuses, or official certificates
- Fake screenshots or location proof
- Making messages “look official” or forged
- Alibis, cover stories, or instructions to lie
- Impersonation or fraud

If you enter risky wording, the app shows a safety warning and suggests a respectful alternative instead.

## What you must not use the app for

- Impersonation of people, employers, schools, or organizations
- Fraud, harassment, threats, or illegal activity
- Creating documents that could mislead others about facts or authority
- Circumventing honest communication in serious situations

## Serious situations

For **serious work, school, medical, legal, or emergency matters**, handle them **honestly and directly**. Contact the right person, supervisor, doctor, or authority as appropriate. This app is not a substitute for professional or emergency advice.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use any message before sending it. Review every message and make sure it is truthful and appropriate for your situation.

## Reporting concerns

If you see a safety issue in the app, use **Settings → Feedback**.
