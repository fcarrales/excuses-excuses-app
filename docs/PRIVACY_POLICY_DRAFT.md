# Privacy Policy Draft — Excuses, Excuses!

**Last updated:** Beta v0.8.0  
**Status:** Draft for beta — not legal advice. Have a lawyer review before public release.

---

## Introduction

Excuses, Excuses! (“the app”) is a message assistant that helps you write respectful, ready-to-send texts for awkward moments. This draft describes how the beta handles your information.

## No account required

You do not need to create an account or sign in to use this beta.

## No backend in this beta

In the current beta, messages are generated **locally on your device** using built-in templates. The app does not send your messages, favorites, or personal notes to our servers because **there is no backend** in this version.

## No ads

This beta does not show advertisements.

## No tracking

This beta does not include analytics or third-party tracking scripts.

## No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal data. In this beta, we do not collect personal data on our servers.

## Local storage on your device

The app stores the following **only in your browser** (localStorage on web):

- Message history and favorites
- Saved people and style presets
- App settings and preferences
- Checklist and UI state (e.g. dismissed cards)

This data stays on the device and browser you use. It is **not** synced to the cloud in this beta.

## Data may be deleted

If you clear your browser storage, uninstall the PWA, or use a different browser or device, your saved data may be **permanently lost**. Use **Export backup** in Settings to save a copy you control.

## Export and import

You can export all app data to a JSON file and import it later. Export and import are **entirely controlled by you**. We do not access these files.

## Feedback email

If you choose to send feedback, you may email us at the address shown in the app (configurable via `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEEDBACK_EMAIL`). Email is **optional and user-initiated**. We only receive what you choose to send.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by your local laws without parental consent.

## Future versions

Future versions may add optional features such as cloud backup or accounts. If that happens, **this policy would be updated first**, and optional features would require your consent before use.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this draft as the app evolves. The in-app version and `/privacy` page will reflect the current summary.

## Contact

For privacy questions about the beta, use the feedback email shown in **Settings → Feedback**.
